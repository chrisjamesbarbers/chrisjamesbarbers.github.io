## Important Update Regarding Appointments

As a small business we truly appreciate the loyalty and support you show Christopher James Barbers. To ensure we can continue to provide the best service possible, we need to address a growing issue.

Lately, we have seen an increase in **"no-shows"** - appointments booked but not attended. Because we operate primarily by appointment, these missed slots result in lost time for our barbers and lost revenue for the shop.

To protect our business, we are implementing the following policy effective from 8th May 2026:

**- 24 Hour Notice:** we require at least 24 hours' notice for any cancellation or rescheduling.

**- Cancellation Fee:** appointments cancelled with less that 24 hours' notice, or missed entirely, will be **CHARGED IN FULL**.

We value your time and ask that you please respect ours. 

Thank you for your understanding and supporting local business.

Kind regards, 
**Chris**
